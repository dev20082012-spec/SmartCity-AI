PRIVACY POLICY & MUNICIPAL DATA GOVERNANCE
COMPLIANT WITH DPDP ACT 2023 & GDPR PRINCIPLES

1. Edge-Based Anonymization and Data Minimization

SurakshaDrishti operates under strict data minimization guidelines. All incoming video feeds are processed in volatile memory (RAM) at edge gateways. Automated algorithms blur recognizable facial features and cryptographic one-way hashes are applied to vehicle registration plates prior to telemetry generation.

2. Rolling Retention and Automated Purge Cycles

Raw video frames that contain no classified incident threats are immediately overwritten in circular frame buffers and are never committed to permanent disk storage. When an active threat is verified (e.g. fire hazard, major collision), only the corresponding 15-second contextual incident clip is stored for municipal review, with an automated 72-hour purge policy unless subpoenaed by law enforcement.

3. Zero Biometric Profiling Commitment

The computer vision models deployed in SurakshaDrishti (YOLOv8 architecture) are strictly trained for object identification (vehicles, smoke, crowd volumes, safety hazards). The system contains zero facial recognition models, demographic classification weights, or behavioral tracking algorithms targeting individuals.

4. Independent Audit Log Verification

All operator actions, dispatch commands, and video inspection events are recorded in an append-only cryptographic ledger. Municipal oversight committees maintain read-only audit privileges to verify compliance with privacy guidelines.

TERMS OF SERVICE : MUNICIPAL PLATFORM OPERATING AGREEMENT
DOCUMENT REF: MUNI-SDR-TOS-2026

1. Authorized Municipal and Emergency Personnel Usage

Access to the SurakshaDrishti platform is restricted to credentialed municipal operators, traffic dispatchers, police emergency command centers, and authorized civic administration personnel. Unauthorized access attempts are monitored and reported in accordance with applicable cybersecurity legislation.

2. AI Decision Support and Human-in-the-Loop Protocol

SurakshaDrishti provides computer-vision-assisted decision support. Automated severity classifications and route recommendations are intended to aid dispatch personnel. All critical interventions, physical agency dispatches, and emergency sirens require verification by a human operator in accordance with the Municipal Command Protocol.

3. Evidentiary Standards and Chain of Custody

Incident logs exported via this system include UTC timestamps, GPS telemetry, and algorithm confidence metrics. When utilized for official traffic collision reports or municipal inquiry, exported files must maintain digital signature validation.

4. System Availability and Service Level Objective (SLO)

The platform architecture guarantees a 99.9% uptime target for telemetry ingestion, with an edge failover time not exceeding 500 milliseconds in the event of primary camera node disconnection.
